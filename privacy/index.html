Privacy

This page explains what information is handled when you use the Saraswat Integrated Services website and its contact form.

Information you choose to send

The contact form asks for your name, email address, enquiry type, subject and message. Organisation or company information is optional.

Contact-form delivery

Contact-form submissions are transmitted through Formspree for message delivery. Please do not use the form to send confidential, highly sensitive or security-critical information.

Analytics and advertising

The current SIS website does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. External websites linked from SIS operate under their own privacy practices.

Purpose of contact information

Information submitted through the form is used to review and respond to the enquiry you send and, where relevant, continue the related business or project discussion.

Questions

For privacy-related questions concerning this website, use the SIS contact form and identify the subject as a privacy enquiry.
